Mahjong Quick Start

by Soul Society
The goal
Be first to make 4 sets + 1 pair

That's 14 tiles. Shout “Mahjong!” and you win.

The 30-second version
  1. Everyone holds 13 tiles. The dealer starts with 14.
  2. On your turn: draw 1 tile, then throw 1 away face up. Say its name.
  3. Want someone's throw-away? Call it if it finishes a set for you.
  4. First to 4 sets + a pair shouts “Mahjong!” and wins.

Tap any section below for the details.

1The 3 shapes you build

Run (“Chow”)

3 numbers in a row, all in the same suit.

3 of a kind (“Pung”)

3 identical tiles. Any tile works, including winds and dragons.

Pair

2 identical tiles. You need exactly one pair to win.

Winds and dragons can't make runs. They only work as 3 of a kind or a pair.
No 4-of-a-kind sets today. If you get a 4th copy, it's just a normal tile. Keep it or throw it away.
2Setting up
  1. Shuffle all the tiles face down.
  2. Build walls. Each player builds a wall 2 tiles high and 18 long. Push the 4 walls into a square or a pinwheel.
  3. Roll the dice to pick the dealer. Roll again at the start of every game.
  4. Take your tiles from the wall: 13 each, and the dealer takes 14.
  5. Flowers? Set them aside and draw replacements (see below).

Flower & season tiles

These aren't played. Any time you get one, at the start or mid-game, put it face up beside you and draw a replacement from the wall.

3Taking your turn

The dealer goes first by throwing away 1 tile. After that, turns move counterclockwise, which means to the player on the right of whoever just went.

  1. Draw 1 tile from the wall.
  2. Throw away 1 tile face up in the middle.
  3. Say its name out loud (“3 Bamboo!”).

Between turns you always hold 13 tiles.

4Calling a throw-away

When someone throws away a tile you need, call it out loud right away, before the next player draws.

“Chow!”Player before you only
Takes the tile to finish a run. Only from the player who went right before you (on your left).
“Pung!”Anyone
Takes the tile to make 3 of a kind. Play continues from you, so others may get skipped.
“Mahjong!”Anyone
Takes the tile that finishes your hand. You win!

After you call, put that set face up in front of you. It's locked in and can't be changed. Then throw away a tile.

5Winning (and nobody winning)

You win by finishing 4 sets + 1 pair with either:

  • a tile you draw yourself, or
  • a tile anyone throws away.

Shout “Mahjong!” and lay out all 14 tiles so the table can check.

Made a mistake? No penalty. Take your tiles back and keep playing.

If the wall runs out

Nobody wins that game. Reshuffle, roll for a new dealer, and play again.
