Mahjong Quick Start

by Soul Society
The goal
Be the first to make 4 sets + 1 pair

That's 14 tiles in total. When you have them, say “Mahjong!” and you win.

Key words
WallThe face-down tiles you draw from.
DiscardA tile you put face up in the middle of the table. “Discarding” means putting one there.
Set3 tiles that go together: a run or 3 of a kind.
Pair2 identical tiles.
CallSaying “Chow!”, “Pung!” or “Mahjong!” to take a discard.
The 30-second version
  1. Everyone starts with 13 tiles. The dealer starts with 14.
  2. On your turn: draw 1 tile from the wall, then discard 1 tile.
  3. Need someone's discard to finish a set? Call it out loud.
  4. The first player with 4 sets + 1 pair says “Mahjong!” and wins.

1The shapes you build

Run (“Chow”)

3 numbers in a row, all in the same suit. Runs only stop at 9: there's no wrapping around from 9 back to 1.

3 of a kind (“Pung”)

3 identical tiles. Any tile works, including winds and dragons.

Pair

2 identical tiles. A winning hand has exactly one pair.

Winds and dragons can't make runs

They only work as 3 of a kind or as your pair.

No 4-of-a-kind sets

We don't use 4-tile sets today. If you get a 4th copy of a tile, it's just a normal tile: keep it or discard it. You also can't call a 4th copy when someone discards it.

2Setting up
  1. Shuffle. Turn all the tiles face down and mix them around.
  2. Build the walls. Each player builds a wall in front of them: 18 tiles long and 2 tiles high (one tile stacked on top of another). Push the 4 walls together into a square or a pinwheel.
  3. Pick the dealer. Each player rolls the 2 dice. The highest total is the dealer. If there's a tie for highest, those players roll again. Roll for a new dealer at the start of every game.
  4. Take your starting tiles. The dealer goes first, then the player on the dealer's right, and so on around the table. Each player takes 4 tiles at a time from the wall, in order, until everyone has 12. Then everyone takes 1 more. The dealer takes 1 extra.
  5. Set aside flowers. If you have any flower or season tiles, put them face up beside you and draw the same number of replacement tiles. If a replacement is also a flower, do it again. Flowers don't count toward your 14 tiles.
Where to draw from: always take the next tile in order, right after the last tile someone took. This is the same whether you're drawing on your turn or drawing a flower replacement.
3Taking your turn

The dealer goes first. The dealer already has 14 tiles, so on the first turn they skip the draw and just discard 1 tile.

After that, turns go counterclockwise: the next turn belongs to the player sitting on the right of whoever just played.

On every turn:

  1. Draw the next tile from the wall.
  2. Flower? Set it aside face up and draw another tile.
  3. Discard 1 tile face up in the middle, and say its name out loud (“3 Bamboo!”).

After you discard, you have 13 tiles, counting any face-up sets in front of you.

4Calling a discard

When another player discards a tile you need, you can call it to take it. You can only call the most recent discard, and you have to call it right away, before the next player draws.

“Chow!”Player before you only

Takes the discard to finish a run. Only allowed from the player who played right before you (the player on your left). The discard can go at either end of the run, or in the middle.

“Pung!”Any player

Takes the discard to make 3 of a kind with 2 matching tiles you're holding.

“Mahjong!”Any player

Takes the discard that finishes your whole hand. You win!

After you call Chow or Pung

  1. Take the discard. Don't draw from the wall this turn.
  2. Put the finished set face up in front of you. It's locked: you can't change it. It counts as 1 of your 4 sets.
  3. Discard 1 tile.
  4. Play continues with the player on your right. After a Pung, anyone in between gets skipped.

You can't call a discard…

  • To make a pair, unless that pair finishes your winning hand.
  • For a 4th copy of a tile you already have 3 of.
  • For a run, unless it came from the player right before you.

If more than one player calls

MahjongbeatsPungbeatsChow

If two players both call Mahjong on the same discard, they each roll the dice. The highest roll wins the tile.

5Winning, mistakes and draws

Winning

You win when your 14 tiles make 4 sets + 1 pair. Your face-up sets count. The last tile can be:

Say “Mahjong!” and lay all 14 tiles face up so everyone can check.

Mistakes

  • Called Mahjong by mistake? No penalty. Take your tiles back and keep playing.
  • Drew or played out of turn? No penalty. Put the tile back where it came from and continue with the correct player.

If the wall runs out

When the last tile has been drawn and discarded and nobody calls Mahjong, nobody wins that game. Reshuffle, roll for a new dealer, and play again.
